- Added survey templates: an analyst defines the questions to ask about an organisational unit or a logical application, in their own words and languages, and what each answer means. Four templates ship ready to use: Customer check, Application intake, Application entitlement review and Unit roster.
- Added *Send survey* on a unit's or application's page: send a survey to a person or to the unit's Accountable, or to every unit below it at once, with an optional due date and message.
- Added *My surveys*: a signed-in person answers the surveys sent to them (a draft is kept as they go); a person without an Identity Atlas role can sign in just to answer when context-owner access is on. An analyst can also fill a survey in on someone's behalf.
- Survey answers become claims under *What we know*, reviewed like interview claims, so a survey and an interview that disagree show up as a contradiction. A survey can ask a question per member or per entitlement; answers about an entitlement are kept as knowledge and never change the crawled data.
