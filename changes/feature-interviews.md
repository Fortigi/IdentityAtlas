- Added the experimental Interviews API (`/api/v1/interviews`, feature flag `interviews`, off by default) for a planned native interview app: look up who a spoken name may refer to (two people with the same first name stay ambiguous instead of one being picked), read the interviewed manager's team and the access its members hold, and keep claims made in an interview together with where in the recording they were said.
- Interview claims are proposals only: an analyst approves, rejects or defers them, and an approval is recorded as a decision without changing any Identity Atlas data. Earlier versions of a claim and their evidence are kept unchanged when it is revised.
- Interviews keep no audio and no transcript on the server; by default only a fingerprint of each quoted excerpt is stored. Interviews are visible only to the person who recorded them, expire after their retention period (90 days by default) and can be deleted, leaving only an audit entry.
