- Added an experimental "Organisation" tab (feature flag `orgTruth`, Admin → Experimental or `FEATURE_ORG_TRUTH=true`) where the organisation's own lists — projects, assets, teams, data domains and their owners — are uploaded, kept as they were given, turned into entities and relations, and linked to the accounts, groups and contexts the crawlers synced.
- The import wizard proposes the object-relation model of an uploaded list, from the local model when available.
- Organisation entities are projected into contexts, so projects and their owners' access show up in the matrix and reports: one context per project, asset or team holding the groups linked to it, and a twin holding the accounts of the people it is related to (such as its owner).
- The Organisation tab can read the organisation model (entity types, relations between them, links to accounts and groups, with counts), list and search entities, and show one entity with its relations, links, source and graph.
- Owners and groups from an organisation list are matched to accounts, people, groups and contexts by e-mail, employee id, name, admin-account prefix or name tokens, each with a confidence score; clear matches are linked at once, ties and proposals below the threshold wait in Review, where an analyst can confirm, reject or move a link, and later imports keep those decisions.
- An import wizard uploads an organisation list, proposes its model, detects how it links to accounts and groups, checks data quality and runs the import; a profile can be repeated as a full or delta import.
- The Organisation tab shows the uploaded sources, the model that was read from them, every entity with its relations and links, and a review queue for proposed links: sources can be downloaded and imported again, the model is drawn as a diagram or a table (and copied as Mermaid), entities are searchable and open in their own detail tab with a relationship graph, and proposed links can be confirmed, rejected or moved to another candidate.
- An uploaded organisation source can be deleted from the Sources tab (after confirmation): its entities, relations, links and import runs go with it and the organisation contexts are rebuilt without it; a source whose import is still running cannot be deleted until it finishes.
- Organisation lists (xlsx/csv) can be uploaded and kept as given, profiled per column, dry-run against an import profile for a data-quality report, and imported in full mode (entities that left the list are closed) or delta mode (nothing is closed); import profiles are versioned.
- In the import wizard, each attribute of an entity can be linked on its own: an owner column to an account, a team column with several people to several accounts, and the entity's own name to a group or resource; the data-quality check reports every such link separately with the number of values it scored, and an entity's name can be given its own attribute name.
- The import wizard proposes the model on its own as soon as the model step opens for a new list; Propose again re-runs it.
