Live demo

Take Identity Atlas for a spin.

A public, no-login demo loaded with a synthetic dataset. Click around, open the access matrix and see for yourself how Identity Atlas makes sense of authorization data — nothing to install, no tenant to connect.

Before you go in

  • It resets regularlyThis environment is restored to a clean state on a regular basis. Anything you change, add or upload can be wiped at any time — treat everything here as temporary.
  • It's shared and openThere is no sign-in — everyone uses the same instance. Other visitors may see, change or remove anything you do while you're in there.
  • The data is made upEverything you see belongs to a fictional company ("Fortigi Demo Corp"). There are no real people, accounts or credentials in here.

Don't upload anything real. The demo lets you import files, but do so entirely at your own risk. Never upload personal, confidential or production data — we take no responsibility for anything you put into this environment.

Once you're in, try this
  1. Open the access matrix and scope it to the Sales department.
  2. Pick a person and see how each permission was granted — directly, through a group, or via a business role.
  3. Look at an identity's risk and how it was built up.
  4. Browse systems, resources and identities to see the whole picture in one place.
