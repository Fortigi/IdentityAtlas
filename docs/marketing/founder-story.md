# Founder Story

> *"Tell me about you. What's your back story? What led you to build this — how did
> you arrive here?"*

That's the question a good investor (or a good conference audience, or a good blog
reader) asks first. Not "what does the product do" — *why you, why this, why now.*
This page is the answer, in the founder's own voice.

The product's own history — FortigiGraph → the v5 PostgreSQL rewrite — lives in
[History](../history.md). **This page is the human story behind it**, and it's the
one that earns attention.
